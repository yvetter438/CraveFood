Common questions

Do you take a cut of affiliate sales? No. You add your own links and keep your commissions.

What counts as an affiliate link? One shoppable URL per video on Free—typically your main shop link or hero product. Pro adds unlimited per-ingredient links.

What counts as a video? Each recipe post on your profile—Short, Reel, or hosted clip.

Can I upgrade later? Yes. Your page and links carry over when you move to Pro.

Is pricing final? Early-access pricing may change; waitlist members get notice before any change.
